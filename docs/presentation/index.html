THE FORENSIC BOTTLENECK

The Attribution Blindspot

On-chain ledgers record every transaction cryptographically, but wallet addresses are inherently pseudonymous.

  • Subpoena Target Gap: Legal freeze requests must be served on regulated Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs), not raw hex hashes.
  • Intermediary Laundering: Bad actors route funds through 2 to 3 intermediary wallets to obscure fund trails.
  • Manual Analysis Fatigue: Manually expanding multi-hop transaction graphs across Etherscan takes hours per case.
REAL INVESTIGATIVE COST

Delayed Asset Recovery

In cryptocurrency crime, time-to-subpoena determines recovery success. When stolen funds enter an exchange, investigators have a critical window before off-ramping to fiat.
